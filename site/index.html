MusicThing

Spotify, with everything else turned off.

A small Windows player for your Spotify library. The Spotify app keeps playing in the background; MusicThing shows your playlists, the queue and the controls, and nothing more.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 and 11

You need
The Spotify app for Windows and a Premium account.
Playback
Runs through Spotify itself, so sound quality and downloads stay as they are.
Updates
New versions download on their own and install when you quit.
First run
The installer isn't signed, so Windows may warn about it. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
